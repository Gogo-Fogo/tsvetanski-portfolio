# MUMOSA Team Alignment One-Pager

**Purpose:** get our team aligned on who we are designing for, what the client currently needs, and what our Axure RP 11 digital prototype should actually prove.

## What The Client Is

MUMOSA is a multimodal situational-awareness dashboard that combines text, images, schema graphs, and simulation.

The published paper's current focus is forensic/post-crisis investigation and training exercises for responders, not a fully deployed real-time field tool.

The future direction does include real-time crisis support, dynamic timelines, danger highlighting, and more agent-like guidance.

## What The Client Needs From A Strong Prototype

- Reduce fragmented information by bringing multiple evidence types into one understandable workflow.
- Help users understand what happened, when it happened, and how different pieces of evidence connect.
- Keep AI grounded in evidence so users can trace claims back to trusted sources.
- Show how schema, source material, and simulation can work together instead of feeling like disconnected features.

## Recommended Team Focus

- Primary user: post-crisis investigator, CSI, fire investigator, or similar analyst role.
- Primary task: reconstruct the event and verify evidence across modalities.
- Dashboard role: search, source comparison, chronology, evidence browsing, and event overview.
- VR role: spatial evidence review, hazard understanding, linked evidence in place, and training support.

## What The Digital Prototype Should Include

- Natural entry into the incident, ideally through open search or incident selection rather than rigid upfront filters.
- An event overview page with AI summary, source cues, key evidence, and a clear next step.
- An evidence detail view that shows what the AI says, where it came from, and what related evidence to inspect next.
- A lightweight timeline or event sequence view.
- An optional handoff into VR as a deeper spatial review mode, not the only investigation path.

## What To Avoid

- Designing separate full journeys for every profession before users even enter the system.
- Making role filters a hard gate at the front door.
- Trying to solve full real-time live sensor fusion in this semester prototype.
- Letting VR replace the dashboard instead of complementing it.

## Alignment Decision For This Week

Agree on one primary user, one main workflow, one incident scenario, and one VR interaction worth demonstrating.

          About

          Named after the wild ox

          ТУР · TURthe aurochs, Bos primigenius

          "I'm an American developer, Bulgarian by nationality. When this app needed a name, I went back to my roots and found the тур: the aurochs, the wild ox that once roamed Bulgaria's forests."

          The ancestor of all cattle

          Bulls stood up to 1.8 metres at the shoulder. People painted them in red ochre, the same pigment as the TUR mark.

          It may have held on longest in Bulgaria

          The last aurochs is usually said to have died in Poland in 1627. A horn core dug up in Sofia in 2020 dates from as late as the early 1700s.

          10% of profits bring the aurochs back

          TUR is named after the aurochs, Europe's wild ox. It was hunted until none were left. Now it's coming back, and part of every subscription helps.

          10%

          Why bring it back

          Big wild grazers like the aurochs keep meadows and forest edges open, which lets many other plants and animals live there. Without them, landscapes grow over.

          Rewilding groups are breeding cattle that look and live like the aurochs from old breeds such as the Maronesa and Sayaguesa, then releasing them into wild landscapes.

            Privacy Draft · final policy at release

            Your training stays on your phone

            TUR has no user account, no advertising SDK and no analytics service. Workouts, notes, bodyweight and progress photos are stored on your iPhone.

            Exercise media

            Bundled exercise images open offline. When you ask for more images or a video, the provider receives ordinary request details such as your IP address. Your workout log is never sent.

            Apple Health and Garmin

            Both are optional. TUR reads and writes only what you allow, and nothing is uploaded to us.

            Exports and backups

            Exports go only where you send them. Full backups include private notes and photos, so keep them somewhere you control.

            This website

            Voting, commenting and requesting features need your email, so each person gets one vote and hears from us when a request ships. Your email is never shown; comments show only its first letter. To limit abuse we count actions per scrambled IP address for 24 hours.

            If you ask to hear about the launch or to test an early build, we use your email for that and nothing else.
